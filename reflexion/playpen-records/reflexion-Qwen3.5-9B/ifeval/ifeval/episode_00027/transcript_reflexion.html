# The Grand Java Immersion: An Extravagant 7-Day Mastery Journey

### **Day 2: The Flow of Data and the Power of Loops**

* **Evening Banquet:** Debugging the chaos. We will use the Eclipse debugger (or IntelliJ's magical red arrows) to step through code line-by-line, pausing time itself to inspect variables and catch the elusive `NullPointerException`.

* **Morning Ritual:** The Arrays are too small for our grand ambitions. We shall summon the `ArrayList`, a dynamic reservoir that grows as needed. We will add, remove, and iterate with the grace of a swan.
* **Afternoon Quest:** The `HashMap` and `HashSet`. Here, we learn the art of the Key-Value pair, storing data with the precision of a Swiss watch. We will explore the `Set` interface to ensure no duplicate elements dare enter our kingdom.

* **Morning Ritual:** The concept of the Parent Class. We will create a generic `Vehicle` and watch as `Car`, `Truck`, and `Bicycle` inherit its traits, standing tall on the shoulders of giants.

* **Evening Banquet:** The `interface` contract. We will define abstract agreements that classes must honor, ensuring that every `Drawable` object knows how to render itself, regardless of its specific shape.
